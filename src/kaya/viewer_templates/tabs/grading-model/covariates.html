Who Climbs Harder?

With gym effects accounted for, the model estimates how much each personal covariate shifts a climber's ability. Ape index (wingspan minus height — a proxy for reach relative to body size) shows a clear, well-behaved positive relationship: more reach for your height helps, roughly quadratically over the observed range.

Gym grading style also comes out clearly once there's enough of a connected network to compare against — some gyms grade noticeably softer or stiffer than others, and the model recovers this as a per-gym correction with real statistical confidence.
