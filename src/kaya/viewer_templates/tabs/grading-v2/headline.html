The Headline

Across a connected network of 29 gyms, the stiffest grader and the softest differ by 1.29 grades — more than a full V-grade. 19 of the 29 differ from the network average by a margin that excludes zero.

Why this is bigger than the previous estimate. Version 1 measured six gyms that all belong to Touchstone. Those six span only 0.25 grades between them — they are a single company with a shared setting culture, so what v1 really measured was within-brand consistency, not gym-to-gym variation. The full network spans 5.2× wider.
