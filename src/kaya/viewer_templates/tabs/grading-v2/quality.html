How much of each climber do we use?

The model reduces every climber-gym pair to a single number: their hardest send. That is 2.76% of all logged sends. It sounds brutal, but 77.5% of sends sit two or more grades below the climber's own max — warm-ups and volume, carrying little information about a ceiling.
