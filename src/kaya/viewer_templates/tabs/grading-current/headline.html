The stiffest gym and the softest differ by more than a full V-grade

Across a connected network of 29 gyms, the stiffest grader and the softest differ by 1.29 grades. 19 of the 29 differ from the network average by a margin that excludes zero — so this is not a handful of outliers dragging a mean around. A correction is the number of grades a gym’s labels sit above or below the network average, measured on the V-scale: positive is stiffer, negative is softer.

The spread is 5× wider than version 1 reported, and version 1 was not wrong — it was measuring something else Settled

Version 1 measured six gyms that all belong to Touchstone. Those six span only 0.25 grades between them, because they are one company with a shared setting culture. What v1 measured was within-brand consistency; what this measures is gym-to-gym variation across four companies. The full network spans 5.2× wider. The v1 page is kept under Archive — v1 Model.
