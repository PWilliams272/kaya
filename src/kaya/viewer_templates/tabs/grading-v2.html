How Hard Is That Gym, Really?

A V5 at one gym is not a V5 at another. This model separates a climber's ability from the gym's grading style, using nothing but the grades climbers already log. Version 2 rebuilds the estimate on 29 gyms across four companies — and finds the gym-to-gym spread is far wider than a single-chain sample suggested.
