A V5 Is Not a V5

Across 29 gyms and four companies, the same climb graded V5 at one gym would be graded more than a full grade differently at another. This is what the send logs say once you separate how hard the climbers are from how hard the gym grades.

Updated 2026-08-05 · 20,014 recorded bests · 10,357 climbers
This is the short version. It carries the results, the model behind them, and the checks that decide whether to believe them. Every alternative that was tried, every diagnostic, and every approach that failed is on Grading Model — Full Detail, which quotes the same fit and the same numbers.
