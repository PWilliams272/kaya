The stiffest gym and the softest differ by more than a full V-grade

Across a connected network of 29 gyms, the stiffest grader and the softest differ by 1.29 grades. 19 of the 29 differ from the network average by a margin that excludes zero, so this is not a handful of outliers dragging a mean around.

A correction is the number of grades a gym’s labels sit above or below the network average, on the V-scale. Positive is stiffer — a climb labelled V5 there plays harder than V5 elsewhere. Negative is softer.

Comparing gyms requires climbers who visit both, and that is the entire difficulty Settled

A gym where everyone tops out at V4 could be grading harshly or could simply have weaker climbers, and one gym’s logs can never tell you which. The only thing that separates them is climbers who appear at more than one gym: if the same person sends V6 at one and V4 at another, the difference is the gym. Every number here rests on that, which is why the network is defined as gyms joined by ≥ 50 shared climbers.
