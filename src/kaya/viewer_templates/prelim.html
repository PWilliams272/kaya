The scores

Each fit is scored by elpd — expected log pointwise predictive density, an estimate of how well the model predicts a climber it has never seen, computed from the draws it already has rather than by refitting ten thousand times. Higher is better. The unit is the climber, not the send: integrating out a climber’s ability offset makes their rows conditionally dependent, so leave-one-row-out no longer exists in closed form. These numbers are comparable to each other and to nothing else on this site.

What each shape says height buys

A coefficient on a squared z-score is not a quantity anyone has intuition for, so here is each fitted form drawn back in inches: the ability a climber gains or loses for their body size. Shaded bands are 89% credible intervals — the range the posterior actually supports, not a standard error. Each curve is centred on its own mean, because the model centres its design matrix and the level of these curves is therefore arbitrary. Only the shape means anything.

The posteriors

Every sampled parameter of the selected fit, with the interval the draws support and the two diagnostics that decide whether to believe them. R-hat compares variation between chains to variation within them — 1.00 is perfect, lower is better, bar 1.01. ESS (effective sample size) is how many genuinely independent draws came out once autocorrelation is accounted for — higher is better, floor 400.

The chains

R-hat and ESS compress a whole run into two numbers, and both are blind to real failures — the sweep before this one saturated its trajectory limit on every single iteration without either diagnostic noticing. Looking at the chains is the cheapest defence. What you want is traces that overlap so completely you cannot tell them apart, with no drift and no chain sitting apart from the others.

The joint posterior

A parameter’s own interval hides the thing that most often makes a fit untrustworthy: two parameters the data cannot tell apart. Those show up as a diagonal ridge, meaning the pair is only jointly identified — raise one, lower the other, and the model fits equally well. Each off-diagonal panel is a genuine joint sample: the draws are thinned with one shared stride, so point i in two panels is the same draw rather than two unrelated numbers.

Every number here is read from runs/traces/ through scripts/build_v2_prelim.py; nothing is hand-typed. Frozen chains are dropped and said so. The published write-ups live at the main viewer.
